E3 Week 2 (Course Week 12) · Lab

Instrumentation Design Session — Beyond-Provided-Hardware Investigation

Students select and justify sensors, DAQ configuration, and signal conditioning for their E3 experiment. A required component: students must investigate at least one sensor or measurement platform not provided in the course — using manufacturer datasheets, professional literature, online sensor databases, or engineering standards — and evaluate whether it offers meaningful advantages for their specific measurement problem. MATLAB uncertainty analysis is used to predict expected measurement performance before any hardware commitment. This is the CLO 6 demonstration in action: not evaluating provided options, but independently identifying and evaluating alternatives.

3 min Assessments 1 source variant(s)

Learning Objectives

  • Select sensors for an original aerospace experiment with written rationale connecting sensor specifications to accuracy requirements and intended use — CLO 1
  • Specify DAQ configuration and signal conditioning strategy for an original experiment, including sampling rate justified by Nyquist argument and anti-aliasing filter cutoff justified relative to signal bandwidth — CLO 1, CLO 4
  • Use MATLAB-based uncertainty analysis to predict the measurement performance of a proposed sensor and DAQ configuration before any hardware is selected — CLO 2
  • Investigate at least one sensor or measurement platform not provided in the course and evaluate whether it offers meaningful advantages for a specific measurement problem — CLO 6
